title: Bali Travel Guide
city: Bali

Bali is an Indonesian island known for beaches, surfing, rice terraces, temples and wellness retreats. Popular areas include Ubud (culture and yoga), Seminyak and Canggu (beaches and cafes), Uluwatu (cliffs and surf) and Nusa Penida (day trips).

Best time to visit Bali is the dry season from April to October, with July and August being the busiest months. The wet season (November to March) has heavy showers, but it is greener and cheaper.

Getting around Bali: renting a scooter is common, but an international driving permit and a helmet are required, and traffic can be chaotic. Ride-hailing apps and private drivers are good alternatives. Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) is the main gateway.

Culture in Bali: dress modestly at temples, wearing a sarong and sash, and never step on the small daily offerings placed on the ground. Nyepi, the Day of Silence, shuts down the entire island for 24 hours, including the airport.

Budget for Bali: about 40-100 US dollars per day depending on the style of stay. Villas with private pools can be surprisingly affordable.
