title: Paris Travel Guide
city: Paris

Paris, the capital of France, is famous for the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame, cafes and fashion. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot and by metro.

Best time to visit Paris is April to June and September to October, when the weather is mild and crowds are manageable. July and August are warm and busy, and many locals leave the city in August. Winter is cold and grey but pleasant for museums and Christmas markets.

Getting around Paris: the metro covers the whole city and a Navigo pass or ten-ticket carnet is cheaper than single tickets. Airports Charles de Gaulle and Orly connect to the centre by RER train or bus.

Museums in Paris: the Louvre, Musee d'Orsay and the Pompidou Centre are the best known. Book timed tickets online to skip queues. Many national museums are free on the first Sunday of the month in the low season, but rules change so check ahead.

Budget for Paris: Paris is expensive, with a typical mid-range budget of 150-220 US dollars per day. Save money with bakery lunches, picnics along the Seine and museum passes. Visitors from outside the Schengen area usually need a Schengen visa.
