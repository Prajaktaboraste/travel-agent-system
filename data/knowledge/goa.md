title: Goa Travel Guide
city: Goa

Goa is a coastal state on India's west coast, popular for beaches, seafood, nightlife and Portuguese-era architecture. North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Anjuna) is lively with beach shacks and clubs, while South Goa (Palolem, Agonda, Colva) is quieter and more relaxed.

Best time to visit Goa is from November to February, when the weather is dry and pleasant with temperatures around 20-32 degrees Celsius. Peak season is December and January, so book hotels early. The monsoon (June to September) brings heavy rain, rough seas and many closed beach shacks, but hotel prices drop sharply.

Getting around Goa: renting a scooter is the most common way to travel and typically costs a few hundred rupees per day. Taxis and app-based cabs are available but can be pricey. Dabolim (GOI) and the newer Mopa (GOX) airports serve the state.

Things to do in Goa: visit the Basilica of Bom Jesus and the churches of Old Goa, explore Fontainhas in Panaji, take a sunset cruise on the Mandovi river, try Goan fish curry, vindaloo and bebinca, and visit the Anjuna flea market.

Budget for Goa: a mid-range traveller can manage on about 50-80 US dollars per day including a hotel, food and local transport. Backpackers can spend much less in hostels and beach huts.
