Check visa rules for every country on your route well before you travel, because processing can take from a few days to several weeks. Official government or embassy websites are the only reliable source.

Your passport should normally be valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date and have two blank pages. Keep digital copies of your passport, visa and tickets in cloud storage or email.

Many countries offer an e-visa or visa on arrival for tourists, but conditions such as stay length, fees and proof of onward travel vary. Carry printed hotel bookings and return tickets because airlines and immigration may ask for them.

Indian passport holders travelling to the Schengen area, including France, need a Schengen visa applied for through the consulate or its visa centre. Apply about 15 days to 3 months before the trip and include travel insurance.
