title: Jaipur Travel Guide
city: Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is called the Pink City because of its terracotta-coloured old town. It is part of India's Golden Triangle along with Delhi and Agra.

Best time to visit Jaipur is from October to March, when days are sunny and cool. Summers (April to June) are extremely hot, often above 40 degrees Celsius, so sightseeing is hard in the afternoon.

Main attractions in Jaipur include Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar (a UNESCO World Heritage observatory) and Nahargarh Fort for sunset views. A combined monument ticket is available for several sights and can save money.

Food and shopping in Jaipur: try dal baati churma, laal maas and pyaaz kachori. Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar are good for jewellery, block-print textiles, blue pottery and leather shoes. Bargaining is expected in markets.

Budget for Jaipur: roughly 40-70 US dollars per day for a comfortable trip. Heritage hotels in restored havelis are a popular, memorable place to stay.
