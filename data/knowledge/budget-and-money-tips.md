title: Budget and Money Tips
city:

Set a daily budget before you travel by adding accommodation, food, local transport and activities, then add a buffer of 10 to 15 percent for surprises. Accommodation is usually the biggest cost.

To save money travel in the shoulder season, which sits between peak and low season, when prices are lower and crowds smaller. Book flights early, compare prices across dates and use public transport instead of taxis.

Carry a mix of payment methods: a debit or credit card with low foreign transaction fees and some local cash. Notify your bank before you travel and avoid exchanging money at airports where rates are poor. ATMs inside banks are usually safer than street ATMs.

Eating where locals eat, choosing set lunch menus and buying groceries for breakfast can cut food costs in half in expensive cities.
