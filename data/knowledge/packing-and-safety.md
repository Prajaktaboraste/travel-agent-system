title: Packing and Travel Safety
city:

Pack light and choose versatile clothes you can layer. Always carry a power bank, a universal plug adapter, any prescription medicines in their original packaging and a small first-aid kit.

Buy travel insurance that covers medical emergencies, trip cancellation and lost baggage, especially for international trips. Read the policy for activities such as scuba diving or motorbike riding, which are often excluded.

Stay safe by keeping valuables in a hotel safe, using a cross-body bag in crowded places, and being wary of overly friendly strangers and unofficial taxis. Share your itinerary with a family member and save local emergency numbers.

Drink bottled or filtered water where tap water is not safe, wash your hands often and check whether vaccinations are recommended for your destination at least six weeks before departure.
