title: Tokyo Travel Guide
city: Tokyo

Tokyo is Japan's capital, a huge city that mixes neon-lit districts like Shinjuku and Shibuya with calm temples such as Senso-ji in Asakusa and the Meiji Shrine.

Best time to visit Tokyo is March to May for cherry blossoms and mild weather, and October to November for autumn colours. Summer is hot and humid, and early summer brings a rainy season in June. Winter is cold but dry and sunny.

Getting around Tokyo: the train and subway network is extensive, clean and punctual. Buy a Suica or Pasmo IC card to tap through gates on trains, subways and buses. Taxis are expensive. Many travellers use a Japan Rail Pass only if they plan long-distance trips.

Food in Tokyo: sushi, ramen, tempura, yakitori and convenience-store snacks are all excellent. Tsukiji Outer Market is great for breakfast. Tipping is not customary in Japan.

Budget for Tokyo: a mid-range traveller should plan about 100-180 US dollars per day. Business hotels and capsule hotels are affordable options, and lunch sets offer very good value.
